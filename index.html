COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Can I access my hosted desktop from outside the office?

Remote access can be designed using secure browser-based or remote desktop methods. The exact setup depends on your applications, network and security requirements.

Can you host Tally Prime or other business applications?

We can review hosting compatibility and configuration requirements. Any software must be used in accordance with its vendor licensing, supported configurations and terms.

Will I be able to monitor my server?

A customer monitoring portal is part of the planned service design. Live metrics and alerts become available after the monitoring backend is connected and tested.

How much does hosting cost?

Pricing depends on CPU, RAM, storage, backup needs, number of users, software requirements and support level. Contact us for a configuration-based quotation.

Do you provide backups?

Backup options can be scoped for your requirements. Before production use, backup frequency, retention, recovery objectives and restore testing should be agreed clearly.
